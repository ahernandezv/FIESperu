# FIESperu: Food Insecurity Experience Scale ('FIES') Estimation for Peru

Downloads, harmonizes, and analyzes microdata from the Food Insecurity
Module ('Módulo de Inseguridad Alimentaria') of the Peruvian National
Household Survey ('ENAHO') conducted by the National Institute of
Statistics and Informatics ('INEI') to estimate the prevalence of
moderate or severe and severe food insecurity using the Food Insecurity
Experience Scale ('FIES'). Implements the official 'INEI' weighted Rasch
calibration, equating to the global reference standard, and combined
sampling plus measurement error estimation under complex survey designs
following Cafiero, Viviani, and Nord (2018)
[doi:10.1016/j.measurement.2017.10.065](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.measurement.2017.10.065)
. Beyond official prevalence estimation, provides a reproducible data
resource for teaching, training, and applied research in food insecurity
measurement, 'FIES' analysis, Rasch modeling, psychometric validation,
and complex survey methodology.
